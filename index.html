Nigeria

"Nigeria; my country is a very big and exciting place in Africa. We have lots of different people living here, and they speak different languages and have different traditions. We also have busy cities, beautiful villages, forests, rivers, and wide open lands. Nigerian people love music, food, sports, partying, and spending time with family. One special thing I like most about Nigeria is how friendly and hardworking everyone is. Even when things are difficult, Nigerians keep trying to keep smiling and laughing. In other words, we never give up. I believe Nigeria is a special country because she is full of life, culture, and amazing people."
